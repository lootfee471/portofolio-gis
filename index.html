01 · About

Two scales, one instinct.

I move between the contour of a city block and the pixel of a satellite image. Trained as an urban planner, I spent four years drafting zoning plans and RTBL guidelines across Yogyakarta, Tangerang Selatan and Jakarta — then turned the same spatial instincts toward data.

Today that means building machine learning and deep learning models that predict crop yield from Sentinel, PlanetScope and EnMAP imagery, engineering vegetation indices like NDVI and LAI, and helping digitize a century of Hungarian soil survey records into a national legacy map.
