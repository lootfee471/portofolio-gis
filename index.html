01 · About

Two scales, one instinct.

I move between the contour of a city block and the pixel of a satellite image. Trained as an urban planner from my bachelor, I spent three (3) years drafting zoning plans and urban design guidelines across Yogyakarta, Tangerang Selatan and Jakarta; then turned the same spatial instincts toward data.

Today that means building machine learning and deep learning models that predict crop yield and classify land use from Sentinel, PlanetScope and EnMAP imagery, engineering vegetation indices like NDVI and NDWI, and helping digitize big-scale Hungarian soil survey database into a national legacy map.
